About

One problem, three angles

For the last decade I've worked on one problem from different angles. How do the right talent and the right opportunities find each other with more accuracy, more trust and less wasted effort?

That question led me to build three things. graph helps data, AI and analytics professionals show what they've actually done. datavruti helps companies hire them. estana helps recruitment agencies do that work at scale.

They solve different problems, but they rest on the same belief. Better information makes better hiring decisions. Whether it's a company hiring, a recruiter trying to scale, or a professional trying to represent themselves accurately, the goal is the same.

Increase signal. Reduce noise.

What I'm building

Three ideas, one belief

  • datavrutiHelps startups, GCCs and enterprises hire exceptional data, AI and analytics talent. We don't recruit for everything. We go deep into one domain, because specialist hiring needs specialist understanding.datavruti.com →

How I got here

Eight years on one problem

  1. 2018

    I came from a tech background and started Multplyr, an HR tech business.

  2. 2021

    Ravi Vyas and I moved into recruitment services. A few people trusted us with their hiring. One client became two, then three.

  3. 2023

    We went all in on technology hiring. It grew the business. Then two of our largest clients left as hiring froze, and close to 80% of the business went with them.

  4. 2024

    We rebranded as datavruti and kept rebuilding with more clients and more services. Getting back took more than two years.

  5. Now

    Building graph and estana alongside datavruti, so the same idea reaches professionals, companies and agencies.

What I believe

Ideas I keep coming back to

Better information makes better hiring decisions.
Companies, recruiters and professionals all decide better when the context and evidence behind the work are visible.
Specialist hiring needs specialist understanding.
That's why datavruti only hires for data, AI and analytics.
Each stage of a business needs a different answer.
Survival, growth and resilience call for different decisions. None of them is right forever.
Revenue is the starting point, not the answer.
Net fee income tells me what the business is really earning.
Manage by outcomes, then understand them.
Separate what a recruiter controls, influences and can't control, and coach from there.
